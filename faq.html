♡ Sizing & Fit

How do I know my nail size?
We offer XS, S, M, and L nail sizes. Measure the width of your natural nails at their widest points and compare your measurements with our sizing guide. Sizing may vary between manufacturers.
What if I'm between two sizes?
If you're between sizes, choosing the slightly larger nail may allow you to gently file the sides for a better fit, provided the nail design allows it.

♡ Application & Nail Care

How long do press-on nails last?
Wear time depends on nail preparation, adhesive type, and daily activities. Adhesive tabs typically provide shorter wear, while nail glue may provide longer-lasting adhesion.
Are press-on nails reusable?
Some press-on nails can be reused if carefully removed and maintained. Reusability depends on the material, adhesive, and condition of the nails.
How do I apply press-on nails?
Start with clean, dry nails. Select the correct size for each finger, prepare your nails according to the instructions provided, and apply your chosen adhesive as directed. Avoid applying nails over irritated or damaged skin.
How do I remove my press-on nails?
Follow the removal instructions for the adhesive you used. Never forcefully pull or pry off press-on nails, as this can damage your natural nails.
